# Chords

A focused place for our church's musicians and singers to find a Song, read its chords and lyrics, and play it in the order the band will use on the day. A stripped-down alternative to Ultimate Guitar, with no content beyond the Songs themselves.

## Language

**Chords**:
The product itself: the app our church musicians use.
_Avoid_: Chord (singular), the app

**Tabs**:
Ultimate Guitar, the service Chords replaces and imports Songs from.
_Avoid_: UG, Ultimate Guitar Tabs

### Songs

**Song**:
The original of a piece of music: its lyrics, its chords placed over the lyrics, and its Sections in their original order. Described by a title (required), an artist, a Key, a tempo, a time signature and Categories (all optional), and identified by its title together with its artist. No two Songs share both. Always editable by any Member.
_Avoid_: Tab, chart, chord sheet

**Section**:
A named part of a Song, such as a verse, chorus or bridge, defined by the original.
_Avoid_: Part, block, verse (when meaning any part)

**Key**:
The musical key a Song is written in, or an Arrangement is played in. Changing it (transposing) changes it for everyone and is recorded in the History.
_Avoid_: Transposition, pitch, personal transpose

**Category**:
A label describing what a Song is, such as "Hymn" or "Christmas". A Song can have several. Any Member can create one. Songs with none appear as "Uncategorised".
_Avoid_: Tag, genre, type

**Import**:
Bringing one Song into Chords from Tabs, by pasting its text or giving its link, with the Member reviewing the detected Sections, title, artist, Key and Categories before it is saved. Pasting is the dependable route; a link is best effort.
_Avoid_: Upload, scrape

### Arrangements

**Arrangement**:
An alternate ordering of a Song's Sections, optionally with a different Key, that leaves the Song itself unchanged. It may repeat a Section or leave one out. Visible to everyone and editable by any Member.
_Avoid_: Version, copy, order

**History**:
A summary log of who changed a Song, its Arrangements or a Setlist, what they changed and when, including Key changes. Viewable outside Play mode.
_Avoid_: Audit log, revisions, versions

**Play mode**:
A focus state that shows only a Song's lyrics and chords, hiding the History and all other information until the Member exits.
_Avoid_: Performance mode, reader view

### Organising

**Setlist**:
A named, user-made, ordered list of references to Songs, for any purpose: an actual service, a grouping by type, or brainstorming for a future event. Several can exist at once.
_Avoid_: Playlist, folder, collection

**Setlist entry**:
One reference to a Song in a Setlist, remembering whether it was added from the original Song or from a particular Arrangement. Opening it shows that view first, and the Member can switch views while reading.
_Avoid_: Item, track

**Songs page**:
The page listing every Song alphabetically, filterable by artist, Key and Category.
_Avoid_: Library, catalogue

**Setlists page**:
The page listing all Setlists.
_Avoid_: Playlists

**Explore**:
The page Chords opens to: recently opened Songs and a search bar.
_Avoid_: Home, feed, dashboard

### People

**Member**:
An approved person who has joined through an Invite link and can sign in, Import Songs, edit Songs and create or edit Arrangements. Members cannot delete.
_Avoid_: User, musician (as a role), singer

**Display name**:
The name a Member chooses at registration and can change on their profile. It is what the History, Arrangements and Setlists show. Members never see each other's email addresses.
_Avoid_: Username, handle

**Invite link**:
A one-time link an Admin gets when approving an email, valid for 7 days. Following it, the person chooses a display name and password and becomes a Member. The Admin copies it to WhatsApp or text; it is also emailed when email can reach the person.
_Avoid_: Signup link, magic link

**Access request**:
A visitor's request to be approved, made from the sign-in page with a name and email. The Admin approves or declines it from a pending list.
_Avoid_: Application, registration

**Revoke**:
An Admin stops a Member signing in and ends their open sessions. Their name stays on the History and on their work, and an Admin can restore them. Different from removing an approved email that never registered, which deletes it.
_Avoid_: Delete, remove, ban (when talking to the owner)

**Admin**:
A Member who also approves who may sign in and has full access, including deleting.
_Avoid_: Owner, superuser
